♡ A letter for Amber ♡


Happy National Girlfriend Day!

I know that technically you aren't my girlfriend yet, but that doesn't change what you mean to me. In every way that truly matters, you have been the person I choose, the person I love, and the person I want by my side.

From standing beside you at concerts, to all the little adventures and dates we've been on. These past 263 days with you have been some of the happiest days I've had.

Whether it was dressing up for a fancy dinner, making our own little plushies, spending hours surrounded by cats, every moment with you has felt special because it was with you.

Some of my favourite memories aren't even the big ones though. They're the cosy nights where it's just us. Watching shows together, eating good food, laughing at games, and getting completely invested in Stardew Valley like it's our own little world.

I love the comfort we have with each other - the way we can be silly, quiet, affectionate, and completely ourselves. Being with you feels easy. It feels like home.

You have brought so much happiness, warmth, and love into my life. I love your kindness, your creativity, your beautiful mind, and the way you care so deeply about the people around you.

I love your smile, your laugh, your little habits, and even the moments when you doubt yourself, because I know you will succeed in the end. You are someone truly special, and inshallah I can spend my life reminding you of that.

I am so excited for the day I can officially call you my girlfriend. Not because a title changes anything about how I feel, but because I want the world to know you are the person I love, the person I want to grow with, and the person I want to make countless more memories with.

Thank you for these incredible 263 days. Thank you for every laugh, every hug, every adventure, every late night, and every little moment that has made this year with you so beautiful.

I cannot wait to see what comes next - more dates, more concerts, more travelling, more memories, and hopefully one day, a lifetime together.

I love you more than I could ever properly put into words, but I hope this is a reminder of just how much you mean to me.

♥

Yours, always,

— Charlie

I might not be the best with my words, but I wanted to show you how much you mean to me... so I wrote you a poem.

I hope you love it as much as I loved writing it for you.
